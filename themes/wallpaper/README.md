# Fmind wallpapers

The branded wallpapers live in [wallpapers/](../../wallpapers/README.md), with SVG sources and PNG exports for desktop, laptop, ultrawide, tablet, phone and square screens. Each uses the circular FΦ logo on the left, `fmind.dev`, and `AI Agents, MLOps & Security` in Google Sans.

Format: [PNG (W3C)](https://www.w3.org/TR/png-3/)

Download the PNG matching your screen and select it in your wallpaper settings. Choose Fit to preserve the full layout. [Social banners](../../banners/README.md) provide platform-specific layouts.
